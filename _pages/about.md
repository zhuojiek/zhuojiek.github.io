Hi! I am a CS undergrad at UC Berkeley. I'm interested in developing general purpose robots for open world manipulation, especially through unified architectures, video diffusion, 3D representations, and VLAs. I'm also interested in agentic approaches and autoresearch.

I'm currently interning at [Robotics Center](https://www.roboticscenter.ai) and work part time at [Code Ninjas](https://www.codeninjas.com). I previously interned at [Stripe](https://stripe.com) and did research on Mixture of Transformers with [Jiaxin Ge](https://gejiaxin.org) in [Trevor Darrell's group](https://darrellgroup.github.io).
