## Review

There was a difficult qualification quiz at the start. I was able to pass largely thanks to auditing Stanford's CS 336 in the summer and had a good idea of the general LLM lifecycle; maybe around 40% passed the quiz. Otherwise it's a chill seminar class, students give presentations, rebuttals, critiques of papers in Sewon's reading list. Heavy, heavy final project: paper suitable for main conference submission.

Difficulty: **4/5**, Fun: **4/5**
