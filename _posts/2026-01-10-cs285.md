## Review

I was hesitant on taking this class, since I was already enrolled in 4 techs, but oh man am I glad I did. Sergey is probably the best technical lecturer in EECS, and I realized that RL turned out to be a far more important topic than I had thought at the beginning of the semester.

I took the class P/NP out of consideration for my existing workload, but in hindsight I definitely should've taken it for a letter grade.
